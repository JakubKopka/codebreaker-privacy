Privacy Policy

Codebreaker: Color Logic for iOS
Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy describes the iOS version of Codebreaker: Color Logic, published by Jakub Kopka / Appstral.

Game progress

The game saves completed and unlocked levels, best times, achievements, in-game points, and the timing of review prompts locally on your device. These game records are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. No account is required to play, and the puzzles work offline.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to offer rewarded advertisements. An internet connection is required for advertising. Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including an advertising identifier when the required permission has been granted, and app- or developer-scoped identifiers.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising data, such as ads shown, and interactions such as taps and video views.
  • Crash information, performance measurements, and other diagnostic data.

This information is used by Google for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, and service performance. It may be associated with device identifiers. Advertising identifiers may be used for tracking and personalized advertising where you have granted the required permissions. Diagnostic information may be processed without being linked to your identity.

Google's collection, retention, and handling of advertising data are described in its Privacy Policy and advertising information. The SDK's data practices are also documented in Google's iOS data disclosure guide.

Your choices

The app requests advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where required. Advertising requests are gated on the SDK's consent status. When privacy options are required, you can revisit them through “Advertising privacy options” on the main menu.

If the app requests permission to track, iOS presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this permission in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Declining optional advertising permissions does not prevent playing puzzles. Advertisements may be limited or unavailable depending on your choices and region.

You can remove locally saved game progress by deleting the app and its data. To manage data processed by Google, use the controls and contact information in Google's privacy policy.

Contacting support

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to include, and use them to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

Children

Codebreaker is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or requests about information sent directly to us, email appstral@proton.me.
